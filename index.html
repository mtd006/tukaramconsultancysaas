Questions owners ask

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. If you can use Google Sheets you can use these systems. Each product has a demo video, and setup is a few clicks: make your copy, deploy, and add your organisation details.

Where is my business data stored?

In your own Google account. Your copy of the system and every record you enter stay in your Google Drive.

What is the difference between one-time and subscription?

A one-time purchase gives you a lifetime licence for that product. Subscriptions (3 months, 6 months or 1 year) cost less upfront and can be renewed from your client dashboard.

How do I pay, and how fast do I get the product?

Online payments are delivered instantly. For UPI or bank transfer, upload your payment reference in the store; once approved you get your licence e-mail, invoice and copy link.

Can I try the software first?

Yes. Most products have a live demo you can open from the catalogue, plus a YouTube walkthrough.

Can the system be customised for my business?

Contact us with your requirement. Many changes, such as fields, reports and branding, can be added.
